Privacy Policy – Money Tracker

Last updated 23 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app Money Tracker: Expense Manager (package com.gebril.moneytracker), version 1.1.0 and later, published on Google Play by Mohamed Gerbil.

In short

  • No ads, and the app never reads your phone's advertising ID. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Your transactions, budgets, goals, wallets, gold & silver and zakat settings are stored in your account on Google Firebase so they sync between your devices and survive a phone change.
  • Shared spaces are optional. What you put in a shared space, with your name there and any receipt photo, is seen by the members of that space. Your personal space is seen by no one else.
  • Usage statistics are on unless you turn them off in Settings › Help and feedback › Share usage data. They never contain your amounts or notes.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from inside the app.
  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, phone number, messages or bank access.

1. Your account

You sign in with an e-mail and password, with Google, or as a guest (an anonymous account with no e-mail). We keep your e-mail address and account ID so you can reach your data; with Google sign-in we also receive the name and profile-photo link Google shares. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them. If a guest later saves their profile to an e-mail or Google account that already exists, the app copies the guest's data into that account (or leaves it behind, your choice) and erases the guest profile.

2. Your financial data

What you enter yourself, such as transactions (amount, category, date, note, wallet), categories, budgets, savings goals, wallets, recurring transactions, your gold and silver pieces (weight, karat, what you paid) and your preferences, is stored in Cloud Firestore under your account (your personal space), where no one else can read it; what you add to a shared space is stored in that space instead (see below). Receipt photos (a Pro feature, available once we switch it on) are compressed on your phone to a small JPEG and uploaded to Firebase Storage, in a folder that only your account can open. The transaction keeps the photo's private link, so the photo is shown wherever that transaction is: to you, and in a shared space to the members who can see that transaction (see below). We do not connect to your bank accounts and do not read bank messages.

Shared spaces (optional). Beside your personal space you can create shared spaces, for example for your family, a trip or a business (creating one needs Pro), or join someone else's with an invite code (free). Each space is a separate book in Cloud Firestore, with its own transactions, categories, budgets, bills and recurring transactions, goals, wallets and gold & silver. Inside a space:

  • All members see the space's name and the name and role (owner, full access or limited access) of every member. Your name there is the one you type when you create or join the space; the app suggests your Google name and you can change it. Your e-mail address is never shown to them.
  • The owner and members with full access see and change everything in the space, including who added each transaction and its receipt photo.
  • A member with limited access sees only the transactions they added themselves (with their receipt photos), plus the space's categories and wallets to file them under.

An invite code works once, for one person, within 7 days; with it we store the space and role it is for, who made it and who used it. The space also stores the date until which its owner's Pro runs, which every member's app reads: after that date the space can be read but not changed until the owner renews. If you leave a space, what you added stays there for the others; when the owner deletes a space, everything in it is deleted for everyone. Your personal space is never seen by anyone else. Anything you add to a shared space can be seen, and copied (for example in a screenshot), by its members, so invite only people you trust.

Zakat calculator. What you type there (money, trade goods, debts, gold and silver weights) is used on your phone for the calculation; only the school and nisab you chose, the reminder switches and your zakat date are saved, on the phone.

3. What stays on your phone

Your language, theme, number and date formats, the space the app shows, your app-lock PIN (stored as a one-way hash that cannot be reversed), reminder settings, your streak and the times you usually add entries (to pick the daily reminder's time), the zakat settings above, the latest gold and silver prices, and the balance summary shown by the home-screen widgets are saved on your device only. Bill reminders, the daily reminder, the weekly summary and the zakat reminders are local notifications created by your phone; none of them goes through our servers.

4. Permissions

  • Internet: for syncing and the services below.
  • Biometrics: only to unlock the app lock; your fingerprint never leaves your phone and we never see it.
  • Notifications and run at start-up: to show reminders on time, even after the phone restarts. Each reminder has its own switch in the app, and the phone's settings can turn them all off.
  • Photos and camera: the app has no photo-library permission. When you attach a receipt, Android's own photo picker or camera opens and only the picture you choose reaches the app.

  • The screens and features you open, and events such as opening the Pro screen or saving a transaction.
  • How features are used: how long adding an entry takes and whether it was saved or closed without saving (only whether something was typed, never what), the look you chose for your home-screen widgets, and whether a gold or silver piece has a price or a date (never the price or the date itself).

  • The week you installed the app and where the install came from (the Google Play install referrer, for example a link from one of our other apps or a link someone shared with you).

Gold and silver prices and exchange rates (Gold & silver, the zakat calculator) are downloaded from two public services, gold-api.com and ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). The request carries nothing about you or your account; like any website, these services see the network address your phone connects from. Links you share from the app (a transaction picture, the app itself, an invite to a space) open our page aligebril.github.io/get/mt, which sends whoever taps them to the right app store; it may count the tap with Google Analytics (only which kind of link and which kind of phone). An invite code never passes through that page: it travels only in the message you send.

6. Subscriptions and purchases

Payments are made through Google Play; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check your Pro subscription, so it receives your in-app account ID and your purchase history from Google Play (product, date and status). Pro belongs to your Money Tracker account, so it works on any phone where you sign in to the same account. Joining a shared space is free; creating one needs Pro, and a space stays open for changes while its owner's Pro runs.

7. Who processes the data

Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Storage, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and App Check, the Google Play Integrity API, and Google Play Billing; see Firebase privacy and Google's privacy policy) and RevenueCat (purchase management; see the RevenueCat privacy policy) process the data above on our behalf, under their terms and security measures. Their servers may be located outside your country. We do not sell data, we do not share it with advertisers or business partners, and the app contains no advertising. We would disclose data only if required to do so by law.

The app also uses Google Play services for in-app reviews and update checks, and opens Google Play when you tap one of our other apps. Those interactions are governed by Google's privacy policy.

8. How long data is kept

  • Account and financial data: as long as your account exists; deleted when you delete the account.
  • Shared spaces: as long as the space exists. When its owner deletes it, or deletes their account, everything in it is deleted. When you delete your account, what you added to the spaces you are still in is deleted with it; what you added to a space you had already left stays there until its owner deletes the space.
  • Receipt photos: deleted when you remove the photo or delete its transaction yourself, and all of them when you delete your account. A photo whose transaction another member deleted, or whose space was deleted, is no longer shown in the app and stays in your private folder until you delete your account.
  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months at Google, then only as aggregated totals.
  • Crash reports: about 90 days.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for billing, refunds and taxes under their own policies, also after the account is deleted.
  • Guest profiles: erased when you sign out of them or move to an account, since they can never be opened again.
  • If you e-mail us, we keep your message and address only to answer you.

9. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and Firebase security rules let each user read and change only their own data and, as far as their role allows, the shared spaces they belong to. Receipt photos sit in each user's own Storage folder and are reached only through the private link saved with their transaction.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings › Account › Delete account deletes your transactions, categories, budgets, goals, wallets, recurring transactions, gold & silver, receipt photos and feedback, the shared spaces you own with everything in them, and what you added to the spaces you are in, then the account itself. Without the app: how to delete your account.
  • Shared spaces: leave one from Settings › Account › Spaces; there the owner can also remove a member, change their role or delete the space.
  • Deleting the account does not cancel a Pro subscription: cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.
  • Usage statistics: Settings › Help and feedback › Share usage data.
  • Export your data: from the Export screen (PDF, Excel or CSV).
  • For any question or request about your data: support.gebril@gmail.com.

We handle requests in line with applicable law, including Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020) and, for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your rights of access, correction, deletion and objection under the GDPR and the UK GDPR.

11. Children

Money Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. A child invited to a shared space with limited access sees there only what they add themselves; the owner can remove them from the space at any time, and the owner or a member with full access can delete anything they added. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
