Delete your Money Tracker account and data

Money Tracker: Expense Manager (package com.gebril.moneytracker), published on Google Play by Mohamed Gerbil. Last updated 16 November 2026.

You can delete your Money Tracker account and all the data linked to it at any time.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  • Open Money Tracker and sign in.
  • Go to Settings › Account › Delete account.
  • Confirm it's you (your password or Google account), then confirm the deletion.

Everything is deleted within seconds and you are back on the sign-in screen.

Option 2: without the app

E-mail support.gebril@gmail.com from the address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Money Tracker account". We delete the account and its data within 14 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • The account itself: e-mail address, name and sign-in method.
  • Transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals and the money saved in them, wallets, recurring transactions, gold & silver and the preferences saved in the account.
  • Receipt photos and the feedback you sent from the app.
  • Shared spaces you own, with everything in them, for all their members. From a shared space you only belong to, you are removed (with your name and role there) and what you added there is deleted.

What is kept, and why

  • Purchase records (product, date, status) at Google Play and RevenueCat, for billing, refunds and taxes, under their own policies.
  • Usage statistics without your e-mail (up to 14 months) and crash reports (about 90 days), then deleted automatically.
  • What you added to a shared space you had already left before deleting the account stays in that space until its owner deletes it (leaving a space keeps your entries there for the others).
  • Settings of the phone itself (language, theme, formats) stay on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Good to know

  • Deleting the account does not cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.
  • A guest profile is erased as soon as you sign out of it.
  • To stop sharing without deleting the account, leave the space from Settings › Account › Spaces; the owner can delete a space for everyone there.
  • To delete particular data without deleting the account, delete it in the app at any time (a transaction, a goal, a wallet…), or write to us with what you want removed.
