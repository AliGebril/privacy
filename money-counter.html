Privacy Policy – Money Counter

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app “Money Counter: Cash Calculator” (package com.gebril.moneycounter), published on Google Play by Mohamed Gerbil.

Money Counter counts cash. It needs no account, asks for no personal information and works offline. This page explains the small amount of technical data the app sends to the services it is built on, why, and how to have it deleted.

1. What stays on your phone

Everything you enter stays on your device and is never sent to us: your counts, the saved history and its notes, targets, denominations, currency and format settings, themes, and the backup files you create. Backup files are written only where you choose to save them (for example your Google Drive or an email you send yourself) and we have no access to them. The app shows a link to our other apps only to users in Egypt; that check uses your phone’s time zone and SIM country on the device itself and sends nothing anywhere.

Firebase also receives your country and language (from your phone’s settings and a masked IP address) so that remote settings can be tailored by region. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, messages or any personal details.

3. Who processes the data

The data above is processed by two providers on our behalf and under their own terms: Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config and Google Play Billing; Firebase privacy information) and RevenueCat (purchase management; RevenueCat privacy policy). We do not sell data, we do not share it with advertisers or business partners, and the app contains no advertising. Data may be processed on servers outside your country. We may disclose data only if the law requires it.

4. How long data is kept

Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months, crash reports for 90 days. Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to keep your Pro purchase working and to meet accounting obligations. Uninstalling the app ends all collection; reinstalling it creates a new installation ID that is not linked to the old one.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete everything stored on the phone from the app itself (clear the history, or uninstall the app). To have the technical data described above deleted from our providers, email us at support.gebril@gmail.com from the phone with the app installed, and we will arrange the deletion with Google and RevenueCat within 30 days. We cannot delete data we cannot identify, so please write from the device in question and mention “Money Counter”.

6. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the data the app sends is technical and contains no personal details.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Changes that matter are also mentioned in the app’s update notes.
