Privacy Policy – Money Counter

Effective 23 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app Money Counter: Cash Calculator (package com.gebril.moneycounter), published on Google Play by Mohamed Gerbil.

In short

  • No account, no personal details, no advertising. The app works offline.
  • Your counts, history, notes, settings and backups stay on your phone.
  • The app sends only technical data: usage statistics, crash reports, random installation IDs, and the record of a Pro purchase.
  • That data is processed by Google (Firebase, Google Play) and RevenueCat on our behalf. It is never sold or shared with advertisers.
  • To have it deleted, email support.gebril@gmail.com.

1. What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device and is never sent to us: your counts, the saved history and its notes, targets, denominations, currency and format settings, themes, and the backup files you create. A backup file is written only where you choose to save it (for example your Google Drive, or an email you send to yourself); we have no access to it. PDF and Excel reports are created on the device and shared only with the apps you pick.

The app shows a link to our other apps only to users in Egypt. That check uses your phone's time zone and SIM country on the device itself and sends nothing anywhere.

3. Who processes the data

The data above is processed by two providers on our behalf and under their own terms: Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config, and Google Play Billing; see Firebase privacy and security) and RevenueCat (purchase management; see the RevenueCat privacy policy). Their servers may be located outside your country.

We do not sell data, we do not share it with advertisers or business partners, and the app contains no advertising. We would disclose data only if required to do so by law.

The app also uses Google Play services for in-app reviews and update checks, and opens Google Play when you tap one of our other apps. Those interactions are governed by Google's privacy policy.

4. How long data is kept

  • Usage statistics: up to 14 months at Google, then only as aggregated totals.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Purchase records: for as long as needed to keep your Pro purchase working and to meet accounting obligations.
  • If you email us, we keep your message and address only to answer you.

Uninstalling the app ends all collection. Reinstalling it creates new random IDs that are not linked to the old ones.

5. Deleting your data

Everything stored on the phone can be deleted from the app itself (clear the history) or by uninstalling the app.

To have the technical data described above deleted from our providers, email support.gebril@gmail.com from the phone that has the app installed and mention “Money Counter”. We will arrange the deletion with Google and RevenueCat within 30 days. Because the data carries no personal details, we can only identify it with the help of the device it came from.

6. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the data the app sends is technical and contains no personal details.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. Changes that affect you are also mentioned in the app's update notes.
